Express your interest   See how it works

Merak

Turkish: curiosity, the wish to find out

Explore. Spend time with art that moves you, whether exhibitions, films, books or music. Note down what you see, what you feel and what you learn.

Abbozzo

Italian: a first rough sketch

Practise. Build a flagship project with a clear goal, and collaborate with others. Ideally it is linked to an open call or competition, but the real aim is to make something that matters to you.

Chemin faisant

French: along the way

Reflect. Meet your mentor, receive honest and kind feedback, and watch your progress take shape through your log and statistics.

What you take away

  • A certificate of completion
  • An overview of your hours and progress
  • A reference letter from your mentor
  • Guidance and feedback
  • A line for your CV and LinkedIn
  • A way to see, and value, the time you spend on art

Why “Meltem”?

The meltem is the summer wind that crosses the Mediterranean, from the Aegean to the Italian coast. It carries things from shore to shore. We like the idea of art travelling in the same way, between people, languages and places. It is also a Turkish given name, which suits a programme shaped by a French mentor, a Turkish fellow and a home in Rome.

“I started this fellowship because I know how much it helps to have someone beside you while you find your direction. I will read your work carefully, give you honest and generous feedback, and be there throughout. You bring the curiosity; I will bring the time and the care.”

Jeanne
